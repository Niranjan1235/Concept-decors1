Hello, we’reConcept Decors

Concept Decors is a Bengaluru interior design studio built on one simple idea: a home should feel like you, from the first glance to the last light switched off at night. Thoughtful layouts, honest materials and details that last.

We design for the way you actually live, whether that is a family apartment in Whitefield, a villa in Sarjapur or an office in Indiranagar. We handle everything from concept and 3D visuals to carpentry, finishing and handover.

Every space tells the story of the people in it.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How early should we get in touch?

Ideally before your possession date or renovation start. Early planning gives time for design, approvals and material selection, so your timeline stays on track.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Bengaluru and work across the city and nearby areas. Share your location when you enquire and we will confirm the details.

Do you handle design and execution both?

Yes. We offer design and turnkey execution together, including civil work, carpentry, electrical, painting and styling, or design consultation on its own.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the size and scope of the project. We share a clear schedule with milestones once the design and quote are approved.

How do we get started?

Send an enquiry with your name, number, project type and preferred start date. We will call you to arrange a site visit and discuss the next steps.
